How it works

  1. Pick a level — Easy, Medium, Hard or Mixed. No account and no sign-up: everything runs in your browser.
  2. Answer 24 questions — four from each of the six categories, in random order. You can move back and forth and change answers any time before submitting.
  3. See instant results — your score, a per-category breakdown and a full review with explanations for every question.
  4. Keep improving — use Practice mode and the Daily Challenge, earn badges, and follow your progress on the Your Progress page.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a real IQ test?

No. BrainGenius is an informal IQ-style reasoning quiz for fun and practice. It is not clinically validated, and your percentage is not an IQ score. A real IQ assessment is administered and interpreted by a qualified professional.

Do I need an account?

No. In this online version everything — the test, practice, daily challenge, local leaderboard and progress tracking — works in your browser with no sign-up at all.

How is my score calculated?

Your score is the percentage of the 24 questions you answered correctly. You also get a per-category breakdown so you can see your strengths.

Can I change my answers?

Yes. Move between questions with the Previous and Next buttons and change any answer before you submit. Submitting asks for confirmation and tells you how many questions are still unanswered.

Is there a time limit?

Only if you want one. Before starting you can switch on an optional countdown timer; when it runs out you will be prompted to submit.

What is the Daily Challenge?

Five questions that are the same for everyone on a given day. Come back tomorrow for a fresh set.

Where is my data stored?

Only in this browser on this device (local storage). Clearing your browser data erases it. Nothing is sent to a server by this online version.

About BrainGenius

BrainGenius – IQ Test & Brain Challenge is a friendly place to exercise your reasoning skills. Our question bank covers six areas that classic reasoning tests draw on: logical reasoning, number series, pattern recognition, verbal reasoning, spatial reasoning and basic mathematical reasoning.

Every question is original and comes with an explanation, so each test doubles as practice. This online version runs entirely in your browser — no account, no downloads, nothing sent to a server.

Honest note: BrainGenius is an informal IQ-style reasoning test for fun and practice. It is not a clinically validated IQ assessment, and your percentage score is not a measured IQ. Scores here cannot be compared with standardised IQ tests.
