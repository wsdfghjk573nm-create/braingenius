How it works

  1. Pick a level — Easy, Medium, Hard or Mixed. No account is needed; guest mode works fully.
  2. Answer 24 questions — four from each of the six categories, in random order. You can move back and forth and change answers any time before submitting.
  3. See instant results — your score, a per-category breakdown and a full review with explanations for every question.
  4. Keep improving — use Practice mode and the Daily Challenge, earn badges, and (with a free account) track your progress over time.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a real IQ test?

No. BrainGenius is an informal IQ-style reasoning quiz for fun and practice. It is not clinically validated, and your percentage is not an IQ score. A real IQ assessment is administered and interpreted by a qualified professional.

Do I need an account?

No. The full test, practice mode and the daily challenge all work in guest mode. An account simply lets you save results, see your progress history and earn badges.

How is my score calculated?

Your score is the percentage of the 24 questions you answered correctly, scored on the server against the question bank. You also get a per-category breakdown so you can see your strengths.

Can I change my answers?

Yes. Move between questions with the Previous and Next buttons and change any answer before you submit. Submitting asks for confirmation and tells you how many questions are still unanswered.

Is there a time limit?

Only if you want one. Before starting you can switch on an optional countdown timer; when it runs out you will be prompted to submit.

What is the Daily Challenge?

Five questions that are the same for everyone on a given day. Come back tomorrow for a fresh set.

Start your test

Choose a difficulty. Every test has 24 questions — 4 from each category — drawn at random, so no two tests are exactly alike.

About BrainGenius

BrainGenius – IQ Test & Brain Challenge is a friendly place to exercise your reasoning skills. Our question bank covers six areas that classic reasoning tests draw on: logical reasoning, number series, pattern recognition, verbal reasoning, spatial reasoning and basic mathematical reasoning.

Every question is original and comes with an explanation, so each test doubles as practice. Play as a guest, or create a free account to save results, follow your progress and earn badges.

Honest note: BrainGenius is an informal IQ-style reasoning test for fun and practice. It is not a clinically validated IQ assessment, and your percentage score is not a measured IQ. Scores here cannot be compared with standardised IQ tests.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026.

What we collect

Guests can use BrainGenius without giving us any personal information. If you create an account we store your display name, email address and a securely hashed password (we never store your plain password). We store your test results so you can view your history and progress.

Leaderboard

If you post a result to the leaderboard, only your chosen display name, your score, the difficulty level and the date are public. Email addresses are never shown. Registered users can opt out of the leaderboard at any time in Dashboard settings.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal data, we do not use your results for advertising, and we do not track you across other websites.

Your control

You can change your display name and privacy settings, or permanently delete your account and all stored results, from the Dashboard at any time.
